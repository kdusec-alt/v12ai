# V1118 system audit — 2026-10-18

Reviewed the deployed main snapshot a860b05aac573b0aab05b93e6c248ad7d70b41c9, including 212 source, workflow and test files.

## Changes

- Market proxy observations now retain price, symbol, currency, provider quote time or an explicit date-only marker, session, completed-session losing streak, 3/5-session returns and MA20 context.
- WTI CL=F and Brent BZ=F are explicitly continuous futures references. They are not labelled spot or exchange settlement prices. Missing quotes remain missing.
- SOX persistent weakness contributes caution even after a small daily move. Taiwan cash index observations join the existing session guard.
- Unknown or stale TAIFEX dates are not replaced with today's date.
- Daily candidate records retain the observation price and separate scanner, conditional-entry and current quote fields.
- Candidate audit projections require matching prediction ID, ticker, market and target session; reject nonfinite prices; retain actual OHLC and observation of missed rises without creating hypothetical fills.
- Daily audit OHLC now comes from one dated price frame, not a mix of current open and older history tails.
- A bounded main-branch CI workflow runs the 100 core regression contracts without remote memory writes or market requests.

## Verification and limitations

- Python compilation passed for the source snapshot.
- 100 core regression tests passed locally, including failure cases and target-session validation.
- The broad baseline suite had 467 passes and 34 failures. The updated suite before the final OHLC regression had 475 passes and the same 34 failing test names; no new failing test names were introduced. Most failures assert legacy UI labels, dimensions or older action schemas. They have not been deleted or relabelled as passing.
- The additional smoke suite had 17 passes and two legacy source-size/string contract failures.
- Live Yahoo requests from this audit environment were rate limited (HTTP 429); therefore independent current SOX and oil price agreement was not verified here. No substitute numerical quote was fabricated.
- End-to-end future close verification still requires the actual target session to finish. Existing historical predictions are not reconstructed after the outcome.
- Recursive strategy generation, out-of-sample promotion and autonomous strategy replacement remain incomplete. The existing bounded learning-validation gate is retained.
- Streamlit remote-memory write credentials and the optional Google Drive service-account archive remain external operational dependencies. This patch does not renew credentials.
